## Findings

- On the main synthetic dataset the planted cause (Data Structures CO2, r ≈ 0.8) ranks first in all four
  sections of DBMS 2024-25 and the decoy (Programming in C CO5, r ≈ 0) is excluded.
- False positives on random data stay at or under 10%, and only in the smallest cohorts.
- Detection needs a strong link and a mid-sized cohort: an effect of 0.4 rarely clears r ≥ 0.40 after
  mark noise, and noise above 1.0 hides even a strong link.
- **Depth is not penalised when strengths are 3.** In the misses at 20–40 students, the depth-2 candidate
  (FD001 CO1 → UP101 CO2 → DN201 CO4) outranked the direct cause, because the PRD's path strength
  (product of strength/3) is 1.0 for both. Options: break ties by depth, or multiply by a depth decay
  (e.g. 0.8 per extra level). Left as specified pending a decision.
